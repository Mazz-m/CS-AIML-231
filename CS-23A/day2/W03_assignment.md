# W03 Assignment — Data: The Fuel
**Due:** next Wednesday in class · **Glossary:** 3 words (Data · Structured data · Unstructured data · Dataset · GIGO · Bias · Data quality)

## Collect YOUR 15-row table
Build a small, real dataset of your own — any countable thing in your life:
- **15 rows minimum**, **at least 2 columns** (what it is + a number/measurement)
- **Units included** — meters, shillings, days, kilograms. Never a bare number.
- Your choice, as long as it is real and you can count it:
  - market prices for one item across 15 days
  - your study hours across 15 days (or 15 subjects)
  - 15 people's heights/ages, 15 cows' weights, 15 crops' yields
  - rainfall for the last 15 weeks, 15 phone calls and their durations…

Write it as a clean table — tidy rows and columns, a title, a date, units. **No internet needed.** A paper table is perfect.

## Deliverable
One tidy table: **15 rows × at least 2 columns, with units and a title.**

## Worth knowing
- This table becomes **the machine's teacher** in the W04 training lab. "No data, no AI" — today's fuel becomes next week's lesson.
- It must be **good data**: complete, correct, consistent, current, and honest. Check your rows before you bring it — GIGO: garbage in, garbage out.
- Your W02 dataset can grow into this — same subject, now a real table. Or start fresh. Either way, make it yours.

## The honesty rule
Do not round away the truth. If a row is missing, leave it missing and mark it. A machine trained on a small HONEST table beats one trained on a big FAKE one. The bias lesson today: check WHO is inside your data — do not leave out the hard-to-count rows.
